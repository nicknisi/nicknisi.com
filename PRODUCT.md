# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is technical leaders evaluating Nick’s judgment, experience, workshops, speaking, or collaboration for their teams. Senior engineers, event organizers, and existing readers are important secondary audiences.

## Product Purpose

nicknisi.com is the canonical public home for Nick Nisi’s work. It establishes his authority in AI-native developer experience, provides evidence through writing, talks, projects, leadership, and teaching, and gives qualified visitors a clear path to explore workshops.

Success means a first-time technical leader can quickly understand who Nick is, what he works on, why he is credible, and where to go next without diminishing the usefulness of the site’s long-running public archive.

## Positioning

Nick works where AI adoption, developer experience, and engineering practice meet. His authority comes from building the systems and workflows teams use, leading DX/AI work, and teaching those practices through hands-on workshops, talks, writing, and community work.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive through articles, talks, referrals, conference appearances, workshop collaborations, and professional profiles. They may be evaluating Nick as a practitioner, teacher, speaker, collaborator, or workshop leader. The site must support quick credibility checks as well as long-form reading.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing public URLs and the Astro content archive.
- Maintain high-quality responsive behavior and readable light and dark modes.
- The primary homepage action is to explore workshops.
- Create a Nick-centered workshop page that accurately presents his role in the shared Claude workshop collaboration and routes qualified visitors to the agreed shared booking flow.
- Typed Void LLC is the legal/business vehicle, not a competing public brand.
- Keep wedding officiating as a small personal detail, not prominent positioning.
- JS Party and Dysfunctional are past work, not current commitments.
- Weekly streaming is current supporting evidence, not the site’s central identity.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, engagement results, prices, availability, or other commercial claims.

## Brand Commitments

- The public brand is Nick Nisi.
- Nick’s little pixel-art “beef guy” (`src/assets/beef_nick.png`) is a retained signature brand mark. It should appear deliberately without competing with real photography or professional authority.
- The voice is direct, technically credible, warm, playful, and opinionated without hype.
- Preserve the recognizable purple and teal/green lineage of Nick’s earlier identity, but the redesign may reinterpret it through Impeccable’s direction process.
- The site must feel specifically authored for Nick, not like a generic AI company, developer portfolio template, or imitation of Zack Proser’s visual identity.
- Avoid mint-green dominance and background patterns that compromise readability.
- Light and dark themes must both feel intentionally designed.

## Evidence on Hand

- A substantial archive of technical writing in `src/content/posts/`.
- Speaking history and structured appearance data in `src/content/data/speaking.json` and `src/pages/speaking/`.
- Current AI-native engineering workshop material in `src/pages/ai-engineering.astro`.
- Photography and event imagery in `src/assets/`, including recent AI Engineering conference and workshop material.
- Public workshop collaboration evidence on Zack Proser’s Claude workshops page, where Nick is presented as an EM in DX/AI at WorkOS, staff engineer, AI practitioner, and co-instructor at AIE New York and London.
- Current role, biography, project, and social data in `src/data/metadata.json`, `src/pages/about.astro`, and related content collections.
- No unverified testimonials, conversion metrics, or client outcomes may be fabricated to fill design needs.

## Product Principles

1. Establish authority before asking for action.
2. Prove expertise through specific work rather than generic claims.
3. Make workshops prominent without turning the entire personal site into a sales funnel.
4. Preserve the archive while changing the perception of its author.
5. Prefer a distinct, authored identity over competitor imitation or category convention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The redesign must preserve semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, readable contrast in both themes, and responsive layouts suitable for mobile and desktop reading.
